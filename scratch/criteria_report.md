## AP Neighbourhood Workspace Policy 2025-2030

scheme_id: ap_neighbourhood_workspace_policy_2025_2030
rule count: 0

eligibility_criteria:
```text
Policy is aimed at remote workers, freelancers, gig-economy participants and small enterprises needing accessible workspace. Individual access, fees, membership rules, facility locations and any concessions depend on implementing agency/operator guidelines; policy approval alone does not create an automatic individual cash entitlement.
```

terms_and_conditions:
```text
This is a policy to develop shared workspace infrastructure and enable remote work, not a direct individual job or guaranteed free-office entitlement. Access depends on a workspace being operational and its notified membership rules. Fees, subsidies, eligibility, operating hours, internet/electricity facilities and booking rules must be confirmed with the implementing agency. ```
```

what_it_does_not_provide:
```text
Does not itself guarantee a WFH job, salary, employer, monthly unemployment allowance, personal laptop or free home broadband. A shared workspace is a place to work remotely; it is not the same as working from one's own home.
```
